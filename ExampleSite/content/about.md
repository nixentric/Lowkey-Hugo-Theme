Hi, I'm **Alex Lowkey** :wave:, a professor of artificial intelligence at the Unknow AI Lab. I lead the Robotic Neurobiology group, where we build robots that reconfigure themselves, swarms of small machines that organize without a leader, and sensor networks that move around on their own.

## What I work on

- **Distributed robotics:** many simple robots working together on a task none of them could finish alone.
- **Mobile computing:** software that keeps working when the network, the battery, or the hardware does not.
- **Programmable matter:** materials that change shape or behaviour on command.

## What I write about

This blog is where I think out loud. Expect three kinds of posts:

1. **Notes from the lab:** what we tried, what broke, and what we learned from it.
2. **Explainers:** research topics written for curious people who do not read papers for fun.
3. **Tools:** the editors, scripts and workflows that keep my day running, including how this site is built with Hugo and the [Lowkey theme](/docs/).
