The features in this section work out of the box. Most need no setup at all; the rest need a line or two of configuration.

1. [Dark mode]({{< relref "dark-mode" >}}): true black, follows the system setting, no flash on load.
2. [Dates]({{< relref "dates" >}}): "3 days ago" for new posts, a localized date for older ones.
3. [Comments]({{< relref "comments" >}}): giscus or Disqus.
4. [SEO & analytics]({{< relref "seo-analytics" >}}): meta tags, sitemap, RSS and Google Analytics.
5. [Translations]({{< relref "translations" >}}): interface text in English, Portuguese, Indonesian or your own language.
