title: "Customization"
description: "Change the look and the templates without forking the theme."
weight: 4
---

Hugo always looks in your own site before it looks in the theme. To change anything, put a file with the same path in your site and Hugo uses yours instead. The theme can then be updated without losing your changes.

1. [Styling]({{< relref "styling" >}}): colors, fonts and widths with Tailwind CSS.
2. [Overriding templates]({{< relref "layouts" >}}): the header, footer, post cards and anything else, plus your own shortcodes and icons.
